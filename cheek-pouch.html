Rules, levels and wheel log

Rules

  • Run left into the burrow to bank. Bigger trips pay much more.
  • A hit spills half your cheeks onto the ground. Grab them back before they fade.
  • Golden seeds add 20. In Free play they fall when the real wheel logs work; in the Daily Burrow, yesterday's wheel work sets how many.
  • Power-ups: a helmet blocks one hit, a magnet pulls seeds in, big cheeks hold 16.
  • In Free play, every bank pays ×2 while the real hamster runs.

Every even level the boss cat swipes three times. Hop them all for a seed jackpot.

Competitions

  • Daily Burrow: the same run for everyone, resetting at midnight UTC. Runs are checked by replaying your key presses. The top three keep a podium.
  • Block Champion: the best Daily Burrow score set while a Squeekchain block is mined wins that block.
  • Free play is practice: it keeps your streak and counts toward run missions, but only verified Daily Burrow runs reach the boards and earn score XP.
  • Streaks: play on back-to-back days for bonus XP.
